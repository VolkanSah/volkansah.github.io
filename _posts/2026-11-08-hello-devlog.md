# Wall of Shames (WoS)
#### **Exposing Fraudulent AI Tools & GitHub/Gitlab Scams**

> A community-driven project documenting and analyzing fake "AI Hacking Tools" and scams (thanks to AI assistance!)
> that exploit non-technical and young users through social media.


> [!CAUTION]
> DO NOT DOWNLOAD OR EXECUTE ANY CODE FROM THIS ORGANIZATION!
> This organization is an **automated evidence archive**: repositories matching scam-related keywords (e.g., Fake WormGPT, DarkGPT, "Uncensored LLMs") are forked here as snapshots.
> Many contain malicious payloads or payment funnels designed to steal credentials and money.
> **Inclusion is not a verdict.** Only repositories marked with the topic `verified` have been reviewed by a human. Unmarked entries may include false positives.
> Owners can request a re-check or removal at any time: NemesisCyberForce ./at/. proton.me

#### How it works
1. **Collect (automated):** a bot searches GitHub for scam-related keywords and forks matches as evidence snapshots.
2. **Review (human):** every find is checked by hand (payment funnels, archives instead of code, fake "custom models", API wrappers with false claims).
3. **Mark:** reviewed cases get the topic `verified`.
4. **Object:** anyone can ask for an entry to be re-checked or removed.

<details> 
<summary>🚨 Documentation & Persistence Protocol</summary>


#### To prevent scammers ...

... from hiding their tracks by deleting repositories or editing deceptive claims after being exposed, Wall of Shames follows a **strict Persistence Protocol:**

### Evidence Preservation:
- 📸 **Immutable Forks:** We create immediate forks of every identified scam repository as permanent "Snapshots of Deception"
- 📋 **Log Archiving:** All admissions of guilt, deceptive marketing, and technical failures are archived as screenshots and Markdown logs
- 🔒 **Archive Links:** Where possible, additional copies via Wayback Machine / Archive.today
- ⚖️ **Timestamps:** Fork creation dates and bot logs document when a repository was captured and are available on legitimate request

### The "Wall of Shame" Mark:
> "You can delete your repo, but you **cannot** delete the record of your actions."

Even if a repository is deleted by the owner or GitHub, the evidence remains accessible on our platform to:
- Inform the community
- Assist law enforcement
- Prevent repeat offenses
- Protect future victims

</details>
